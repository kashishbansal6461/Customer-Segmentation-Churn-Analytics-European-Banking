# Executive Summary

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes churn behavior in a European retail banking customer base. The objective is to identify churn-prone customer segments using demographic, transactional, and behavioral variables. The analysis supports retention strategy design and hypothesis testing for customer relationship management.

## Business Context

Banks collect large volumes of customer data but often struggle to convert it into actionable retention insight. A single overall churn rate is not enough, because churn risk varies by customer segment. The project segments customers by geography, age, balance, tenure, and engagement to understand where the risk is concentrated.

## Key Insights

- Churn risk differs materially by geography, age group, and customer tenure.
- Inactive and lower-engagement customers are more likely to leave.
- High-balance customers may have different exposure patterns and deserve targeted monitoring.
- Retention strategies are more effective when based on segment-level evidence rather than aggregate averages.

## Recommended Actions

1. Prioritize retention outreach to high-risk segments.
2. Focus on recently acquired and inactive customers.
3. Review country-specific retention tactics.
4. Use segment dashboards for campaign planning and monitoring.

## Conclusion

The project provides a practical framework for understanding churn in European banking. It helps teams make informed, evidence-based decisions that reduce customer loss and protect long-term revenue.
